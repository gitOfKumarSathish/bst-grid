## When to use it

A footer that shows total / filtered row counts and, when a range is selected, live aggregates of
the selected numbers.

## How it works

Renders total and filtered counts always; with a cell range selected it adds
`runtime.getSelectionStats()` — **sum · avg · min · max · count**.

## Gotchas

- The selection aggregates need **`enableCellSelection`** (row/filtered counts show regardless).
- Chrome — appears in the settings sheet ("Display").
