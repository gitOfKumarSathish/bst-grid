### When to use it

Add visible **Undo / Redo** buttons to the toolbar for users who don't reach for Ctrl+Z.

### How it works

`showUndoRedo` renders the two buttons and follows `enableUndoRedo`; the keyboard shortcuts work
whether or not the buttons are shown.

### Gotchas

- No-op without `enableUndoRedo`.
- The buttons disable themselves at the ends of the history stack.
