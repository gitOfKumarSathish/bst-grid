### When to use it

Show how many rows are selected and offer a one-click Clear.

### How it works

Renders a `{n} selected` chip + **Clear** in the toolbar; it follows `enableRowSelection`.

### Gotchas

- No-op without `enableRowSelection`.
- It reflects **row** selection, not cell-range selection (that count lives in `showStatusBar`).
