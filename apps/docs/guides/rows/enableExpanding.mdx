## When to use it

Master-detail — click a row to reveal a full-width panel with more detail, sub-records, or a nested
table.

## How it works

Adds a leading expander column and renders your `renderDetail(row)` as a full-width row beneath the
expanded one. Control which rows can open with `getRowCanExpand`.

## Gotchas

- Row virtualization **yields** to expanding (detail rows need real DOM), so long lists with many
  open panels are heavier.
- The detail panel is your React — a nested `<BstTable>` is fine for a different column set.
